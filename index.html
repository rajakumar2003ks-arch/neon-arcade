How to Play Free Games on Neon Arcade

Choose any game and press Play Now. The games run in your browser, so you do not need to install an app or create an account. On a computer, use the keyboard or on-screen controls where available. On a phone, use the on-screen buttons and tap controls.

🎯 Reaction Games

In Click Rush, tap the target before the 15-second timer ends. In Whack-a-Mole, tap the mole before it disappears. Try to improve your personal best.

🧩 Puzzle Games

In Memory Match, reveal two cards at a time and remember their positions. In 2048, combine equal number tiles using the direction buttons or arrow keys.

🕹️ Arcade Games

In Neon Snake, collect food and avoid walls and your tail. In Space Shooter, move and fire at enemies. In Brick Breaker, keep the ball bouncing with the paddle.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Neon Arcade games free?

Yes. The games on this page are free to play in your browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No app download or account is required to play these browser games.

Are my scores saved online?

No. Personal XP and supported best scores are saved in this browser on your device. They are not shared across devices or shown on a public leaderboard.

Can I play on a phone?

The page is designed to adapt to smaller screens. Some games have on-screen buttons. If a game control does not respond on your device, refresh the page and try again.

About Neon Arcade ⚡

Neon Arcade is a free mini-game website. Play directly in your browser on a phone or computer. XP, achievements and high scores are stored locally in this browser. They are not a shared online leaderboard.

Contact

Have feedback, found a bug, or want to suggest a game? You can open a public issue on the project repository. Please do not post passwords, private information, phone numbers, or your home address. Send feedback on GitHub.

Privacy

Neon Arcade does not require an account to play. XP and supported personal scores are stored in your browser using local storage; clearing browser data may erase them. This page currently has no Google Analytics or advertising script installed. If analytics or advertising is added later, this notice must be updated to explain the services used and any relevant cookie or advertising choices.
